- **AWS bolts business context and an AI vulnerability service onto its agent stack** — At its New York Summit (June 17, coverage running into the weekend), AWS framed the core blockers to enterprise agents as missing business context and missing security, and launched two services aimed at both. AWS Context auto-builds a governed, access-controlled knowledge graph from a company's databases, documents, emails and chat plus business rules, then exposes it as a runtime agentic-search layer so an agent only ever sees what its caller is allowed to see. AWS Continuum is a model-agnostic vulnerability-management service that discovers from existing backlogs and scans, prioritizes by reachability and production impact, then builds a working exploit in a sandbox to prove a finding is real before recommending and re-validating a fix, leaning on frontier models including Claude Mythos to reason through attack paths (gated preview). Bedrock AgentCore also added Guardrails that screen agent actions for prompt-injection, harmful content and sensitive-data exposure before they execute. Availability and capability claims are AWS's own. A small share of beneficial-trait data folded into an otherwise standard RL post-training pipeline reinforces six traits (truthfulness, epistemic humility, corrigibility, transparent reasoning, fairness, and concern for human well-being) on realistic healthcare, education, science, law and engineering conversations.
