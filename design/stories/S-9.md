# S-9 — Timeline (the self scope as a time series)

I open the timeline view and see my recent weeks as a time series of
commitments, outcomes, and interventions, and can jump from any point to
the underlying record. Interventions and mandate windows have no records
until manda integration (Phase D+); their lanes are reserved and labeled.

Setup: scratch vault, `just up`, then seed (all against :8720):

    curl -s -X POST /self/commitments -H 'content-type: application/json' \
      -d '{"slug":"tl-a","title":"timeline A","started_at":"2026-06-29"}'
    curl -s -X POST /self/commitments -H 'content-type: application/json' \
      -d '{"slug":"tl-b","title":"timeline B","started_at":"2026-07-01"}'
    curl -s -X POST /self/edges -H 'content-type: application/json' \
      -d '{"type":"informed_by","from":"self/commitment/tl-a","to":"world/prediction/x","to_label":"seed headline","author":"user"}'
    curl -s -X POST /self/outcomes -H 'content-type: application/json' \
      -d '{"commitment_slug":"tl-a","component":"observable","result":"confirmed"}'

Pass, on /timeline (the outcome's `recorded_at` is server-assigned "now",
so which week carries the ● depends on the execution date; the multi-week
spread including empty gap weeks is pinned by `timeline.test.ts`):

- the seeded weeks appear: ○ opening markers for tl-a and tl-b, one ●
  close marker for tl-a, and a provenance heat step on the strip for the
  opening week;
- the edges lane counts the seeded edge;
- the two reserved lanes render with "no records yet (Phase D+)";
- clicking the tl-a marker lands on home with tl-a highlighted
  (`/?focus=self/commitment/tl-a`).
