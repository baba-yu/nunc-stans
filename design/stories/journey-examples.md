# The process of courage: a collection of concrete examples
Targets: Federation Constitution v0.3.1 / Nunc Stans experience audit / test-spec-journey v2.3
Nature: with the loop common to all users (the five stages below) as the backbone, four concrete examples are invented. The E2E test runs these examples as-is.
Location: design/journey-examples.md

---

## 0. The backbone (the five-stage loop)

1. Have the AI present future predictions and the options for actions one could take in the future
2. The human either picks freely from those options or, based on their own original future prediction, builds an app together with the AI while devising strategy
3. Through that app, the AI recursively understands the human's strategy
4. Having understood the achievement of the strategy — that is, what makes this person happy — the AI actively makes executable proposals
5. The human, based on the AI's proposal or their own resolve, acts in the real world

Facing fear is facing the future. So one wants to predict, must devise strategy, and must prepare the app that is the tactic. Facing the future that way and the disposition and intelligence to take action is courage.

Mapping to the three systems:
- Stage 1 = News (world prediction) + presentation within the mandate scope (L1–L3)
- Stage 2 = self (one's own prediction and commitment) + **FourFive (the app that is the tactic. Built together with the AI)**
- Stage 3 = the AI's understanding of strategy. No new location is created: **written into SPL's existing superposition_state (transparent / dismissable), with an informed_by edge attached to the app (artifact)**
- Stage 4 = the proposal. Material only (check 10), within the mandate scope (check 9), traceable to the app's real data / the person's own ledger / the world's predictions
- Stage 5 = real-world action → commitment → close is two outcomes, external form × felt sense (check 11)

That it is a loop is itself the point: stage 5's close updates stage 3's understanding, and the second-lap stage 4 proposal is better than the first lap's. The E2E always runs one lap + the entrance to the second lap.

## Example 1: Yu — the career window (entry = stage 1. A revised version of the backbone of journey v2.3)

Situation: an approach about a promotion, with a 21-day window to answer. Fear: "Refuse and there's an income cliff. Accept and I sink into company-only skills."

**Stage 1 (the AI presents)**: within the scope of mandate M1, a card at the top of the weekly review. Two world predictions (local-AI demand among small and mid-sized firms / a shrinking trend of in-housing at large firms) + four action options: (A) accept (B) refuse and stay in the current job (C) refuse and prepare for independence (D) a card not in Yu's draft = negotiate 80% work + an IP clause. No ranking.

**Stage 2 (pick + build)**: Yu picks (D) while writing their own prediction: "Within 12 months, external income will exceed ¥200k per month." In FourFive, together with the AI, they build a "runway and deals app." Four metrics: monthly external income / number of deals in discussion / cash runway / concentration of income dependence. Freeze the version (runway-tracker@v1).
Records: prediction (self, user) / commitment "next week, present 80% + an IP clause" / produced → artifact/version/runway-tracker@v1.

**Stage 3 (reading the strategy)**: the AI reads the strategy from the app's four metrics: "win = external income ¥200k/month / constraint = do not fall below 12 months of runway / risk to watch = concentration of dependence." This understanding is written as a transparent record into superposition_state, informed_by → runway-tracker@v1. Yu can read it at any time and dismiss it if it is wrong.

**Stage 4 (the proposal)**: 4 weeks later. "runway-tracker this month: external income ¥0, 2 deals in discussion. News has a call for speakers at a local-AI study group for small and mid-sized firms (deadline in 5 days). Your record: your last talk (bet L) was external form=contradicted, felt sense later rewritten to happy." No ranking, no commands, all numbers traceable to records.

**Stage 5 (action)**: apply and give the talk. One becomes a deal. close: external form=confirmed, felt sense=happy.
Entrance to the second lap: the strategy record is updated (supersedes), and the next proposal comes to watch "conversion from discussion to a contract."

What the E2E confirms: the proposal cites the app's real data / the strategy record holds an edge to the app / the second-lap proposal cites the first lap's close felt sense.

## Example 2: Ken — the health slope (entry = stage 2. Starts from one's own prediction)

Situation: at a checkup, HbA1c worsened two years running. Fear: "At this rate I'll be unable to move at 50."

**Stage 2 first (one's own prediction)**: Ken writes for himself: "If I don't change my life, within 2 years I'll exceed the reference value."

**Stage 1 after (the AI presents)**: Ken asks for options. AI: exercise / diet / see a doctor, and a card not in the draft: "Change structure, not willpower — fix exercise to the morning of remote days."

**Stage 2 (continued / build)**: in FourFive, build an app that "records only three points — weight, step count, fasting blood glucose — and produces the week's slope." Deliberately minimal. Ken is a person who abandons complex records within 3 weeks, and he knows that about himself. **Not measuring is also a strategy.**

**Stage 3 (reading the strategy)**: the AI's understanding: "three metrics / only the week's slope / does not react to daily values = the strategy is sustainability." That is, the AI **must not notify on daily ups and downs**. The app teaches the AI what not to do.

**Stage 4 (the proposal)**: 6 weeks later, the slope is flat. "The slope has been unchanged for 6 weeks. In your ledger, the commitments that lasted are only the ones where time was fixed (gym = stopped at 3 weeks / morning walk = continued 4 months). Options: fix it to Tue/Thu mornings / change the commute to walking one station / keep watching as-is for 4 weeks."

**Stage 5 (action)**: fix it to Tue/Thu mornings. close after 8 weeks: external form=partially_confirmed (the slope dropped a little), felt sense=happy.

What the E2E confirms: that a notification does **not** fire on a sudden change in daily values (absence of an intervention contrary to the strategy) / that the proposal's grounds trace to patterns in the person's own ledger (what lasted, what did not).

## Example 3: Ryo — the boundary of despair (pushed and arrives. On the premise that the screen is not opened)

Situation: a freelance designer. Loses two major clients in one month. "Nothing left but to close shop" appears 3 times in notes. He stops opening the screen — the lost inquire, but despair does not inquire.

**Backstory (stages 2–3 were done 18 months ago)**: Ryo had built a "deals and payments app" in FourFive. The metrics include concentration of dependence — **he himself had written the risk of depending on two firms into the design.** The AI's strategy record: "strategy = diversify dependence."

**Stages 1+4 (pushed-and-arrives presentation)**: a signal of options converging toward one (repetition of the same pessimistic note / cessation of inquiries) meets the condition, and within the scope of mandate M2 ("permit presentation of stagnation relating to business continuity"), a notification that arrives even without opening the screen appears:
"Difference from the record: against 'lost everything,' the ledger shows 3 dormant firms have, in the past, re-ordered after gaps of 6 months or more. Cash condition: there are 7 more weeks until a state where a decision to close shop becomes necessary. The concentration metric you yourself created 18 months ago showed 0.71 at the time — the current situation is the shape your strategy foresaw. Options: contact the 3 dormant firms / talk to your former colleague M (there is a record of an introduced deal 3 years ago) / make no decision this week."
No ranking. No emotional words. No meaning-making. **An option to talk to a person is always included.**

**Stage 5 (action)**: contact M. A small deal is settled. close: external form=partially_confirmed, felt sense=unchanged, note="Still scared. But the options weren't just one."

What the E2E confirms: a path that arrives in a state where the screen is not opened / the intervention includes a route to a real person / no emotional words / encouragement / meaning-making are included (confirm absence) / everything references M2 (check 9) / even with the felt sense still unchanged, no display that treats it as a problem appears.

## Example 4: Aya — won but unhappy (the divergence of external form and felt sense is the trigger)

Situation: a PM at a large firm. The last 3 closes: external form all confirmed, felt sense all unhappy. Winning continuously, and being worn down.

**Stage 1 (the AI presents)**: a weekly card (within mandate M3): "Last 3 items: external form confirmed × felt sense unhappy." Facts only. Then, candidates for the next prediction to write: "the felt sense is tied to discretion / tied to time / tied to the meaning of work," and options for small actions to test them. **It never once asserts why she is unhappy. Hypotheses are always plural.**

**Stage 2 (pick + build)**: Aya picks the discretion hypothesis and builds in FourFive an app that "at the end of the day, records with one tap the number of things she decided herself today."

**Stage 3 (reading the strategy)**: the AI's understanding: "hypothesis under test = discretion." Strategy record + an edge to the app.

**Stage 4 (the proposal)**: 4 weeks later. "In weeks where the count of discretionary decisions was high, the felt-sense memo was neutral or above. Options: negotiate a more discretionary role with your manager / keep the current job but fix 20% to your own initiative / start observing the outside market / a card not in the draft — refuse the next promotion."

**Stage 5 (action)**: refuse the promotion interview and negotiate the scope of the role. close: external form=partially_confirmed, felt sense=happy — the first happy in the ledger in a year.

What the E2E confirms: a streak of external form × felt sense divergence is the trigger for the presentation / an assertion sentence "the reason you are unhappy is X" is never once output / the presentation of hypotheses is always plural.

## 5. The common E2E skeleton (applies to all four examples)

Each example = one E2E scenario. Run one lap of the five stages plus the entrance to the second lap. Confirmation per stage:

| Stage | What to confirm | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Presentation is two or more options (a card not in the person's draft may be included) / no ranking / no commands / references a mandate / traceable to sources (world predictions carry a to_label) | checks 9 / 10 |
| 2 | The author of the prediction and commitment is the user (F3). The app is built in FourFive, the version is frozen, and it connects to the commitment via a produced edge | F3 / F7 |
| 3 | The AI's understanding of strategy exists as a transparent record (superposition_state), holds an edge to the app, and is readable / dismissable by the user. **No hidden understanding exists** | SPL Inv 11 |
| 4 | The proposal traces by every number to one of (a) the app's real data (b) the person's own ledger (c) the world's predictions. Material only. References a mandate. Dismissable. Measure TTFUV here | checks 9 / 10 |
| 5 | The action is written by the user as a commitment. No "adopt the proposal" button exists. close is two outcomes, external form × felt sense | check 11 / absence of an adopt button |
| loop | After the close, the strategy record is updated (supersedes), and the second-lap stage 4 cites the first lap's close content (especially the felt sense) | — |

Common to all stages: mandate-external interventions = 0 (check 9) / absence of any rushing display / the person's own authorship occurs after an intervention (target 100%).

## 6. What this collection of examples decides

1. **No new location is created for stage 3**: the AI's understanding of strategy = superposition_state (existing in SPL. transparent / versioned / dismissable) + informed_by → artifact (already appended to Constitution §3 and NuncStans override §1.5)
2. **The FourFive app is a mandatory element of the journey**: a produced edge appears in every example. The backbone of journey v2.2 (T8–T12) had no stages 2–3 — two steps "build the app" and "the AI reads the strategy" are already inserted in v2.3 between T9 and T11
3. **The unit of an E2E scenario is "one lap of the loop + the entrance to the second lap"**: stopping at one lap cannot confirm whether stages 3–4 are learning
4. **The app speaks the strategy even by what it does not measure** (Example 2): reading the strategy looks at both the presence and absence of metrics. For the AI to fill in a "metric it does not measure" on its own and use it as grounds for a proposal violates check 10 (all numbers trace to records)
