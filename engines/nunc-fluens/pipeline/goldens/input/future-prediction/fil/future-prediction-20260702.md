Ngunit walang router-only LoRA o router-aware advantage-decomposition na preprint, walang signed-skill-registry interop o NIST non-human-identity control profile, at walang physical-AI production-loop closure na lumitaw. Kaya sumulong ang substrate na bahagi habang nananatili para sa hinaharap ang formal-governance at real-world-deployment na mga patunay.
